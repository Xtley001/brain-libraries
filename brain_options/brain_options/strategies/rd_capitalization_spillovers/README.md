# R&D Capitalization & Technology Spillovers Strategy

## Overview
Based on seminal research by Lev & Sougiannis (1996), Hirshleifer et al. (2013), and Bloom et al. (2013), this strategy captures the systematic mispricing of intangible R&D capital and technology spillovers across intra-industry supply networks.

## Formula Archetypes
1. **R&D Asset Capitalization Intensity**: Measures cumulative R&D investment scaled by balance sheet asset size.
2. **R&D Revenue Conversion Efficiency**: R&D to sales conversion momentum conditioned on options implied volatility.
3. **Sector R&D Leadership Spillover**: Intra-industry R&D divergence isolating technology spillover winners.
