# Capital Investment & Asset Growth Anomalies Strategy

## Overview
Based on Titman, Wei, & Xie (2004) and Cooper, Gulen, & Schill (2008), this strategy exploits the structural negative relationship between aggressive balance-sheet growth, abnormal capital expenditure spikes, and subsequent cross-sectional equity returns.

## Formula Archetypes
1. **Total Asset Growth Inversion**: Penalizes firms with excessive 1-year total asset growth.
2. **Abnormal Capex Spike Penalty**: Flags abnormal spikes in capital expenditure relative to revenue baseline.
3. **Capex Payoff Efficiency Ratio**: Identifies firms delivering superior revenue growth per unit of capex deployed.
