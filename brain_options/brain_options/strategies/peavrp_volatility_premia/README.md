# Post-Earnings Announcement Volatility Risk Premium Drift Strategy

## Overview
Synthesizing post-earnings announcement drift (PEAD - Ball & Brown 1968) with options variance risk premia (Bollerslev et al. 2009), this strategy exploits the structural overpricing and subsequent rapid compression of implied volatility following corporate earnings announcements.

## Formula Archetypes
1. **Post-Earnings VRP Compression Drift**: Exploits delta IV - HV variance crush interaction with EPS shocks.
2. **Earnings Volatility Dislocation Followthrough**: Captures equity drift during extreme implied vs realized volatility dislocations.
3. **Earnings Skew-Revenue Interaction**: Integrates call/put implied skew with top-line quarterly revenue acceleration.
