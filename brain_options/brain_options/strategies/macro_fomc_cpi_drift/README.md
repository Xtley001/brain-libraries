# Macroeconomic & Scheduled Event Drift Strategy

## Overview
Based on findings by Savor & Wilson (2013) and Lucca & Moench (2015), macroeconomic announcements (FOMC rate decisions, CPI releases) account for an outsized portion of cumulative equity risk premium, accompanied by predictable IV compression cycles.

## Formula Archetypes
1. **Macro Event Beta Risk Drift**: High-beta assets capturing disproportionate systematic risk compensation.
2. **Pre-Event Volatility Compression Drift**: Equity relief rallies driven by fast compression of implied uncertainty.
3. **Macro Spread Volatility Normalization**: Cross-sectional return mean reversion conditioned by options volatility surfaces.
