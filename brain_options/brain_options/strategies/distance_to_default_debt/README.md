# Structural Credit Risk & Distance to Default Strategy

## Overview
Rooted in Merton's (1974) structural credit risk model and Bharath & Shumway (2008), this strategy evaluates default probabilities by contrasting corporate debt liabilities against equity market value and options-implied asset volatility.

## Formula Archetypes
1. **Structural Distance to Default**: Direct proxy of equity solvency buffer divided by implied asset volatility.
2. **Net Debt Coverage Improvement**: Deleveraging momentum via reduction in net debt to operating earnings.
3. **Leverage Volatility Insolvency Risk**: High-conviction short candidate identifying fragile, high-IV leveraged companies.
