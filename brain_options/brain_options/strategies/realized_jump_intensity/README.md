# Realized High-Frequency Jump Intensity & Skew Strategy

## Overview
Based on Amaya et al. (2015) and Nonejad (2013), this strategy decomposes equity price variance into continuous diffuse variance and high-frequency jump shocks, trading the resulting market overreaction and implied-realized skew divergences.

## Formula Archetypes
1. **Realized Jump Range Discontinuity**: Measures intraday range jump expansion over realized volatility base.
2. **HV Term Structure Momentum Jump**: Captures sudden term structure shifts in short vs medium realized volatility.
3. **Realized-Implied Skew Interaction**: Exploits cross-sectional divergence between option skew and return trajectories.
